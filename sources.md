# Sources and research layers

The website is designed to preserve the independently created tracker while allowing comparison against external evidence. External sources are **benchmarks and research inputs, not ground truth**.

- **User completed 2026 Brownlow tracker** — primary human/visual 3–2–1–0.5 layer.
- **AFL.com.au Brownlow Predictor** — official-site independent predictor sanity check: https://www.afl.com.au/brownlow-medal/predictor
- **ESPN 2026 Brownlow Predictor** — independent fractional-vote benchmark: https://www.espn.com.au/afl/story/_/page/POINTSBET20242/afl-2026-brownlow-medal-predictor-tracker-leaderboard-odds-every-vote
- **AFL 2026 Brownlow statistical rule change** — https://www.afl.com.au/news/1469154/afl-introduces-approved-player-performance-statistics-to-assist-brownlow-medal-voting
- **FootyWire 2026 fixture and match statistics** — https://www.footywire.com/afl/footy/ft_match_list?year=2026
- **AFL Tables** — historical and game-by-game statistical cross-checking: https://afltables.com/afl/stats/2026.html
- **AFL Coaches Association votes / AFL.com.au** — match-impact validation where publicly available.
- **Wheelo Ratings** — independent probabilistic comparison: https://www.wheeloratings.com/afl_brownlow.html
- **Public AFL discussion / tracker communities** — qualitative disagreement and blind-spot detection only; never treated as authoritative vote data.

## Research principle

Where an external predictor disagrees materially with the human/model count, the model preserves the disagreement unless match evidence supports changing it. The objective is not to converge to the market or public consensus; it is to identify where the independent assessment is genuinely different.

## 2026 fixture mapping

The round/opponent mapping used in the interactive player profiles is sourced from FootyWire's 2026 AFL Fixture page: https://www.footywire.com/afl/footy/ft_match_list?year=2026

The repository also includes `data/fixture-2026.csv` containing the 207 home-and-away matches used for the mapping.
